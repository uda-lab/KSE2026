\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We formalized Leray--Hopf weak-solution existence on $\mathbb{T}^3$ and
$\mathbb{R}^3$ in Lean~4 and mathlib. At the pinned release, the two
kernel-checked capstone theorems depend on no project axioms.

The development record shows why project reliability must be assigned
to more than the proof kernel. The incidents mark boundaries between
mathematical intent, the public declaration set, and reliable project
execution. They motivate the synthesized end-state harness and candidate
practices for similar formalization projects. Because the evidence comes
from one supervised campaign, the effectiveness of these practices remains
to be tested.
