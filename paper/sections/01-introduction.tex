\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

Large language model agents are increasingly used for work that extends
beyond a single proof-search episode. A campaign spanning many issues,
branches, and reviews must preserve mathematical intent as well as
kernel-checked proofs. These are different obligations. The kernel
checks a declaration against its type; project governance must also
decide which type should be proved, preserve the intended public
declaration set through refactoring, and verify the operational record on
which progress reports depend.

This paper studies those obligations in a six-week formalization of
Leray--Hopf weak-solution existence for the homogeneous incompressible
Navier--Stokes equations. The central artifact is a kernel-only
Lean~4~\cite{moura2021lean4} development on
mathlib~\cite{mathlib2020}, with capstone existence theorems on the
periodic three-torus and on $\mathbb{R}^3$~\cite{leray1934,hopf1951,
lerayhopf2026repo}. Both theorem statements are given precisely in
Section~\ref{sec:formalization}. % CLAIM: CLM-001
A July 2026 literature and repository survey found no earlier
machine-checked existence theory for a nonlinear fluid PDE in Lean, Coq,
or Isabelle/HOL.\footnote{The search terms, dates, and nearest results
are recorded in the related-work notes.} This is a survey-conditioned
claim, not a proof that no such formalization exists. % CLAIM: CLM-006

The nearest formalized-analysis results address important adjacent
problems rather than this existence theorem. They include the
Gagliardo--Nirenberg--Sobolev inequality in
mathlib~\cite{vandoorn2024gns}, a Lean formalization of
De Giorgi--Nash--Moser elliptic regularity
theory~\cite{armstrong2026degiorginashmoser}, and an AI-assisted
formalization of well-posedness for the linear kinetic Vlasov
equation~\cite{miller2026vlasov}.

Project-scale precedents provide a second comparison. Human teams used
blueprint-driven coordination in the Odd Order Theorem and the Liquid
Tensor Experiment~\cite{gonthier2013oddorder,commelin2023abstraction}.
TauCeti uses human governance and a standing review rubric for an
AI-authored Lean library~\cite{tauceti2026}. % CLAIM: CLM-010
The present study instead
examines one deep formalization under a single scientific owner and
retains incident-level and session-level evidence about the development
process.

Agentic theorem-proving systems are commonly evaluated through proof
search, benchmarks, or interactive assistance
~\cite{xin2025deepseekproverv2,yang2023leandojo,
deepmind2025alphaproof,zhou2026leanatlas}. Studies of autonomous task
horizons and agent-authored pull requests motivate longer-term
reliability questions~\cite{metr2025longtasks,agenticprs2026security}.
Our unit of analysis is different: a complete mathematical project whose
implementation, reviews, incidents, and resource use can be traced to
preserved artifacts.

This paper makes three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A completed, kernel-only Lean~4 and mathlib formalization of
  Leray--Hopf weak-solution existence on $\mathbb{T}^3$ and
  $\mathbb{R}^3$. The formal result and its exact scope are independently
  inspectable.
\item A synthesized end-state harness design that routes issue-scoped
  changes through checks matched to each changed artifact and records
  operational and evidentiary state. Named incidents document both failures
  that motivated controls and defects caught by applicable controls.
  % CLAIM: CLM-005, CLM-007, CLM-008, CLM-009
\item An evidence method for agent-assisted research that combines
  session-log escrow, claim-to-evidence verification, and cost
  reconciliation. It supports auditable statements about the campaign
  while making coverage gaps explicit. % CLAIM: CLM-002, CLM-003
\end{enumerate}

Section~\ref{sec:formalization} states the two capstone theorems.
Section~\ref{sec:workflow} presents the resulting harness.
Section~\ref{sec:incidents} analyzes the incidents used to assess its
boundaries. Section~\ref{sec:discussion} gives qualified recommendations
and limitations, and Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes.
