\title{Formalizing Leray--Hopf in Lean~4 with AI Agents:\\
Harness Design and Reliability Lessons}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous Author(s)}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Long-running, agent-assisted formalization introduces reliability
questions outside the proof kernel: the intended meaning of a statement,
the preservation of the public declaration set, and the state of the
development process. We report a kernel-only Lean~4 and mathlib
formalization of Leray--Hopf weak-solution existence for the homogeneous
Navier--Stokes equations on the three-torus and on $\mathbb{R}^3$. To our
knowledge, a literature and repository survey found no earlier
machine-checked existence theory for a nonlinear fluid PDE in Lean, Coq,
or Isabelle/HOL. From controls introduced at different stages of the
six-week campaign, we reconstruct an end-state harness in which
issue-scoped changes pass through checks matched to each changed artifact, operational
controls, and evidence capture. Four documented incidents delimit those
controls: they include statement-scope defects, an omitted public declaration,
and an operational resource failure. We present these observations as a case
study rather than a causal evaluation of agent orchestration. The associated
evidence method preserves redacted session, repository, verification, and
cost records and links manuscript claims to their supporting artifacts.
\end{abstract}
